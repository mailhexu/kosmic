# ABINIT — Scoped MPI Source Project

This Project is the same-vault source of provenance and newly gathered evidence for ABINIT MPI CG-reduction guidance only. It does not replace the external full ABINIT Project, and it must not absorb unrelated ABINIT knowledge, specifications, or skill projections.


## Purpose

Provide a bounded, validated source boundary for future distillation of the MPI CG-reduction recipe packet. Historical material is retained as evidence; a shared procedure remains prohibited until current execution evidence supports it.

## Scope

- MPI distribution invariants for reductions over `cg`.
- Communicator and FFT-distribution knowledge needed to explain those invariants.
- Fresh evidence from future ABINIT topology-matrix execution.

## Non-goals

- Full ABINIT Project migration.
- A generated Usage/Development Skill Pair.
- Shared Domain Library publication.
