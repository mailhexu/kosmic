# NC PAO DFT+U derivation audit


## Overview

The self-contained derivation and audit is stored at `papers/DFTU_NC/math/dftu_nc_derivation_and_audit.md`. It distinguishes the NC PAO representation from PAW augmentation machinery and records only source-backed implementation claims.

## Principles

- The local occupation is formed from weighted PAO projections, and every energy derivative contracts the corresponding potential with an occupation derivative.
- Ground-state stress is an analytical PAO strain derivative. It must not be described as a self-consistent strain Sternheimer implementation.
- Source presence and numerical validation are distinct evidence levels.

## Details

Collinear NC DFT+U implements the Dudarev correction and the shared general FLL/AMF kernels. The Dudarev equations are

$$
E_U=\frac{U-J}{2}\sum_s\left[\operatorname{Tr}n^s-\operatorname{Tr}(n^s)^2\right],
\qquad
V_U^s=(U-J)\left(\frac12\mathbf1-n^s\right).
$$

The source computes PAO force and stress derivatives through `forstrnps_dftu`. Spinor ground state uses the combined orbital-spin occupation and applies the same trace functional, but is restricted to `nspinor=2`, `nspden=4`, `istwfk(:)=1`, and no spinor MPI.

For Sternheimer response, the atomic-displacement source includes the PAO derivative, first-order potential, and adjoint PAO derivative. DDK uses the PAO wavevector derivative with ground-state $V_U$ only. A homogeneous electric field keeps PAOs fixed and uses the response potential generated by first-order wavefunctions. The response driver explicitly rejects `nspinor=2`; no NC DFT+U strain Sternheimer source is admitted, although ground-state stress exists.

Runtime smoke evidence is now available: the direct `build_wann` executable completed `v10/t111` through `v10/t115` with the bundled NC pseudopotentials, covering Dudarev, FLL, AMF, alternative AMF, and FLL-no-spin-XC ground-state configurations. The maintained validation record additionally documents scalar-insulating finite-$q$ atomic-displacement response for Dudarev and FLL q/$-q$ fixtures. This does not extend support to metallic or non-collinear DFPT.

The validation document names finite-$q$ fixtures `v10[116-128]`, but no audited branch registers them. Their reachable histories and dangling objects also lack the files. The historical inputs and outputs survive as untracked artifacts in `../abinit_dev/.tmp/nc_dftu_dfpt/`, including `mpi_t117/t117.abi` and `nc_t116_all_dirs.abi`; these recover experiments but not a permanent regression fixture.

The actual feature baseline is `origin/DFTU_epc` at `a442ea21bb`. It was checked out temporarily for source comparison; its audited NC DFT+U source files are byte-identical to those in the original `ProjWannPAO` checkout, so the prior source analysis remains applicable.

The executable SymPy audit validates five mainline algebraic invariants: the real-symmetric collinear Dudarev potential returned by the shared helper; the moving-projector product rule and the `forstrnps_dftu` force/stress contraction; the Hermitian packed-spinor Dudarev functional; the ground-state band correction; and the collinear FLL double-counting derivative. It deliberately does not validate finite-$q$ full-interaction response, AMF derivatives, or a numerical finite-difference result.

The executable suite now has a perturbation coverage map for atomic displacement, DDK, homogeneous electric field, strain, and finite-$q$ phonons. It verifies a product-rule/contraction identity for each implemented derivative class; finite-$q$ additionally enforces the q/$-q$ Hermitian trace and real paired-energy relations. The suite's deliberate mutants cover missing adjoints, conjugation/sign errors, missing q/$-q$ conjugation, and loss of imaginary trace content. This is symbolic evidence only: self-consistency, Berry-phase/DDB assembly, numerical finite differences, strain Sternheimer response, and full-interaction finite-$q$ response remain outside its proof boundary.

Fortran-to-SymPy review on 2026-08-11 matched the collinear Dudarev helper, packed-spinor Dudarev energy/potential, force/stress contraction, atomic-displacement source, DDK source, electric-field source, and strain ground-state boundary. Finite-$q$ review verified scalar admission, complex response storage, response/pair-energy calls, and full-interaction guarding, but did not trace the complete q-star/time-reversal reconstruction proving $n^{-q}=n^{q\dagger}$; retain that item as partial source-review evidence.
